## Show Notes

Episode 1 of The Long Way Here, with co-hosts Olivia Pan and Nina Tseng. Olivia
was laid off from her Director of Analytics role at Nike after 7.5 years;
Nina was laid off from Affirm in a 500-person round three years ago and has
since built a career-coaching business. They compare notes on the two
timelines side by side — the actual HR call, the first week that feels fine
and the second week that doesn't, and the uncomfortably honest answer to "who
defines you: the job, or you?"

Nina walks through how she went from layoff to launching her coaching brand —
Ikigai, *Designing Your Life*, three to four months of pre-work, six months of
training. Olivia is earlier in it: back on the job hunt, thinking about
building AI-driven passive income on the side, and admitting she got so deep
into job-search mode her husband had to sit her down and tell her to slow
down.

If you're in the middle of this — or you will be — this one's for you. Feel
all the feels. It's a very unique experience.

her coaching practice? Leave it in the comments.
